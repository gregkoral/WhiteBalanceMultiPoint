PRIVACY POLICY
White Balance Multi-Point (Android app, package pl.whitebalancemp.app)

Effective date: October 12, 2026
Developer: Grzegorz Koralewski
Contact: koralewski.g@gmail.com


1. SUMMARY

White Balance Multi-Point does not collect, store on any server, share or
sell any personal data. All image processing takes place entirely on your
device. The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no
user accounts.


2. IMAGES YOU OPEN

The app only accesses images that you choose yourself using the standard
Android file picker. It does not scan or read any other files on your
device.

The selected image is processed locally on your device (white balance,
brightness equalization, levels and crop). The image is never uploaded,
transmitted or sent anywhere.


3. IMAGES YOU SAVE

When you tap "Save", the app writes a new JPEG file to the Pictures/White
Balance MP folder on your device. Your original image is never modified or
deleted. Saved files belong to you and remain on your device until you
delete them.


4. PHOTO METADATA (EXIF)

When saving, the app copies the metadata (EXIF) of the original image into
the new file, for example the capture date, camera model and exposure
settings. If the original image contains a GPS location, that location is
also copied into the saved file. This information stays inside the file on
your device; the app does not read it for any other purpose and does not
send it anywhere. Please keep this in mind if you share saved files with
others.


5. DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

To open the file picker in the folder of the last image you used, the app
stores a single reference (a system link) to that image in its private
storage on your device. This reference is never transmitted and is removed
when you uninstall the app or clear the app's data.


6. PERMISSIONS AND INTERNET ACCESS

The app does not request any runtime permissions. Access to images is
granted only for the files you select in the system file picker. The app
does not send any data over the internet.


7. THIRD PARTIES

The app does not use any third-party services for advertising, analytics,
crash reporting or user tracking. No data is shared with third parties.


8. CHILDREN

The app is a general-purpose image editing tool and does not knowingly
collect any information from anyone, including children under the age
of 13.


9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same
location with a new effective date.
